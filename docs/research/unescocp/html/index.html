Welcome to the UNESCO Center for Peace

The UNESCO Center for Peace promotes peace, justice, and human rights through education, culture, and the sciences. Peace is more than the absence of conflict; it requires social justice, equality, and respect for diversity.

We aim to create a culture of peace that transcends borders and promotes global understanding and cooperation.

UNESCO Center for Peace Launches Its International Publishing House

Under the leadership of President Guy Djorken, the UNESCO Center for Peace has officially launched its International Publishing House, a landmark initiative to amplify voices for peace, justice, and human rights on a global scale.

Deputy Director, Ambassador Emmett Imani has been appointed to build and lead the Center's Global Publishing Program, bringing distinguished diplomatic experience and a deep commitment to international education and cultural exchange.
